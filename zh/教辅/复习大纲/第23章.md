## 二、重点公式

### ReAct 单步
$$\text{Thought}_t, \text{Action}_t = \text{LLM}(\text{History}_{t-1})$$
$$\text{Observation}_t = \text{Env}(\text{Action}_t)$$

### ToT 评估
$$V(s) = \text{LLM-eval}(s)$$

### Self-Consistency
$$\hat{y} = \arg\max_y \sum_{i=1}^N \mathbb{1}[y_i = y]$$

### Elo (Arena 排名)
$$R_{\text{new}} = R_{\text{old}} + K(S - P_{\text{pred}})$$
